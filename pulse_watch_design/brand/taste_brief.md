## 3. Brand Identity and Visual Direction

### Brand name

AURA

### Brand positioning

AURA is a premium fitness intelligence platform for people who want to train smarter, recover better and build consistent habits.

### Design style

Create a polished, premium and modern interface.

Use:

- Clean layouts.
- Minimalist design.
- Generous spacing.
- Soft rounded cards.
- Subtle borders.
- Smooth transitions.
- Elegant typography.
- Clear visual hierarchy.
- Data visualization.
- Responsive layouts.
- Light and dark themes, with light mode as the default.

### Color palette

- Primary background: warm off-white (#F7F8FA).
- Primary text: deep charcoal (#15171C).
- Secondary text: muted grey (#8A9099).
- Primary accent: vivid lime (#C7F36B).
- Secondary accent: muted mint (#B8E5D1).
- Recovery color: green.
- Sleep color: lavender.
- Activity color: orange.
- Card background: white.
- Dark mode background: deep charcoal.

Use accent colors sparingly and consistently. Avoid excessive gradients, unnecessary shadows or overly colorful dashboard cards.

### Typography

Use a modern sans-serif font such as Inter, Geist or Manrope.

- Large bold typography for important scores.
- Medium weight for section headings.
- Regular weight for descriptions.
- Small muted labels for chart legends and supporting metrics.
