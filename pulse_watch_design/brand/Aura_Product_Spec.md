# AURA — Smart Fitness Wearable & Performance Platform

## 1. Product Vision

AURA is a premium smart fitness wearable ecosystem designed to help people understand their recovery, sleep, daily activity, training intensity and overall fitness patterns.

AURA consists of a screenless smart band and a companion mobile and web application. The band continuously collects example biometric and movement signals throughout the day and night. The app transforms these signals into simple, meaningful insights that help users make better training decisions.

The product should feel modern, premium, intelligent, calm and personal. Instead of overwhelming users with numbers, AURA helps them understand how their body responds to exercise, rest and everyday activities.

The application should combine the visual quality of a premium fitness product, the clarity of a professional analytics dashboard and the simplicity of a modern mobile wellness app.

All hardware specifications, biometric measurements, user profiles and analytics in this project are fictional. They are illustrative product-design data, not tested hardware specifications or medical advice.

## 2. Hardware Product — AURA Band

Design a minimalist screenless smart fitness band.

### Physical design

- Screenless wearable band.
- Lightweight, comfortable and minimalist design.
- Soft silicone strap.
- Smooth, rounded sensor module.
- Premium matte finish.
- Adjustable strap suitable for different wrist sizes.
- Water-resistant design concept.
- Available in charcoal black, stone grey and soft sand.
- Small optical sensor module positioned on the inner wrist side.
- Subtle AURA branding.
- No display, buttons or unnecessary physical components.

### Sensor concept

The band concept uses:

- Heart rate.
- Heart rate variability (HRV).
- Skin temperature trends.
- Movement and activity.
- Sleep-related movement patterns.

### Battery concept

- Target battery life: approximately 7 days.
- Magnetic charging concept.
- Battery status displayed in the app.

These are design goals, not verified hardware performance.
